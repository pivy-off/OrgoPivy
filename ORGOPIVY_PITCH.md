# OrgoPivy: The Complete Organic Chemistry Learning Platform

## The Problem

**Organic chemistry is the #1 course that makes pre-med students drop out of their dreams.**

Every year, hundreds of thousands of students struggle with organic chemistry. They face:
- **Overwhelming content**: 13+ complex topics across two semesters
- **Lack of practice**: Limited access to quality problems with instant feedback
- **Poor study tools**: Fragmented resources across textbooks, YouTube, and flashcards
- **No personalization**: One-size-fits-all approaches that don't adapt to individual learning needs

**The result?** Students spend 20+ hours per week studying, yet still fail exams. Professors are overwhelmed with office hours. And medical schools lose qualified candidates.

---

## The Solution: OrgoPivy

**OrgoPivy is the all-in-one organic chemistry workbench that transforms how students learn, practice, and master organic chemistry.**

We've built the first platform that combines:
- **Comprehensive curriculum** covering all 26 topics in OrgoChem I & II
- **AI-powered practice** with instant feedback and personalized problem generation
- **Interactive tools** for mechanisms, NMR analysis, and structure drawing
- **Intelligent note-taking** with AI Q&A that searches through uploaded materials
- **Integrated learning** with curated YouTube videos, flashcards, and study guides

**Think Khan Academy meets Duolingo, but built specifically for organic chemistry.**

## Key Features

### 🎯 **Comprehensive Curriculum**
- **26 topics** across OrgoChem I & II, each with:
  - "Must Know" checklists with detailed explanations
  - Step-by-step study guides
  - Curated YouTube video integration
  - Topic-specific practice problems

### 💪 **Practice & Exam Mode**
- **Unlimited practice problems** with instant feedback
- **Auto-graded multiple choice** questions (no manual grading needed)
- **Practice exams** with 10+ problems per topic
- **Achievement system** to gamify learning
- **Progress tracking** across all topics

### 🧠 **Interactive Learning Tools**
- **Mechanism Canvas**: Visualize and understand reaction mechanisms step-by-step
- **NMR Studio**: Upload and analyze NMR spectra with AI-powered interpretation
- **Flashcards**: Smart memorization system with categorization
- **Chemistry Drawing Canvas**: Practice drawing structures and mechanisms

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Study Assistant**
- **Upload your notes**: Students can upload class notes, textbook summaries, or study guides
- **Ask questions**: AI searches through uploaded materials to provide personalized answers
- **Source references**: Every answer includes citations so students can verify information
- **Topic-specific focus**: Ask questions within specific course/topic contexts

### 📊 **Progress & Analytics**
- **Study streaks**: Track daily study habits
- **Progress charts**: Visualize improvement over time
- **Achievement badges**: Unlock rewards for milestones
- **Performance analytics**: See strengths and weaknesses across topics

### 🎨 **Modern UX**
- **Dark mode support**: Study comfortably day or night
- **Responsive design**: Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Fast performance**: Built with Next.js and optimized for speed
